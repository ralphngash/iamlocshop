Our story

Meet the founder

Iyana Thomas — natural hair pioneer, licensed cosmetologist & instructor, Spectrum Award winner, and advocate for education and excellence.

Since 2005

Iyana Thomas

Established in 2005, Iyana began her career as a professional braider in Federal Way, WA. Working alongside chemical stylists, she built her clientele on a passion for natural hair that started at age 13. Born and raised in New York, she was drawn to intricate design — translating patterns from sweaters, trees, and blueprints into braids shaped to each client's head.

In 1997 she began braiding as a hobby; since January 5, 2005 she has been a full-time natural stylist. From booth rental to cosmetology school (BJ's Beauty & Barber College), she represented her school in the Reign of Style competition. Her salon won the Spectrum Award for Excellence in Customer Service. She holds an Instructor's license and serves on the Washington State Board of Cosmetology as the representative for Hair Design.

The name "I AM"

The name comes from a desire to work under the influence of one of the highest names — found in Exodus 3:14. While planning a business with her husband, Iyana combined their initials (I and M). The Spirit led her to "I And M," which became I AM.

"I AM WHO I AM… I AM has sent me to you." — Exodus 3:14

Sent from New York to Washington, the name fit perfectly for entities that celebrate who we are by enhancing natural beauty.

What we built

Today the I AM family includes multiple full-service salons, the Northwest's first Natural Hair College, and Loc Journey products. All specialize in the care, styling, and maintenance of every natural hair texture — type 1 through 4C, processed or natural.

Stylists and students are trained for natural and chemically treated hair. Salons and schools offer top-quality services, retail, and education in professional, inviting atmospheres.

Iyana remains committed to constant education, mastering the craft, and giving her best from consultation to finishing touches — and beyond.

"I believe the shampoo service lays the foundation for the client's overall experience. Most customers look forward to it and use this to rate their entire visit. Also, with my children and me being 'tender headed,' I always take the condition and comfort of the client's scalp into consideration."

— Iyana Thomas, Founder

Called to multiply

After going natural in 2005 and educating clients about the benefits of natural hair — and the harsh reality of certain chemicals — demand grew by word of mouth. Iyana was reminded that "the harvest truly is plentiful, but the laborers are few." She yearned for a place with teamwork, mandatory education, growth opportunities, and a solid natural-salon foundation. That vision became I AM Natural Beauty — stepped into by faith, and still growing.
