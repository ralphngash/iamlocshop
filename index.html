Our story

I AM — beauty with purpose

The name I AM is rooted in Exodus 3:14 and the founders' initials — a subtle honor to faith, family, and calling. Established in 2005 by Iyana Thomas, we grew from a booth rental into multiple full-service salons, Northwest's first natural hair college, and Loc Journey products.

"The shampoo service lays the foundation for the client's overall experience."

We welcome every texture Our Creator made — processed or natural, type 1 through 4C — in professional, inviting spaces built on teamwork, education, and excellence.
